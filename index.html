Discover our
destinations

  1. 01

    Berat Castle
    & Old Town

    The “city of a thousand windows”: Ottoman houses stacked beneath a castle that people still live in.

  2. 02

    Krujë Castle
    & Old Bazaar

    Skanderbeg's mountain fortress and a cobbled bazaar full of handmade crafts.

  3. 03

    Valbona Valley
    National Park

    Alpine peaks, a glacial river and quiet guesthouses in the Accursed Mountains.

  4. 04

    Ulcinj · Kotor · Budva

    Along the Montenegrin coast: walled old towns, the Bay of Kotor and Adriatic beaches.

  5. 05

    Theth National Park
    & Blue Eye

    A remote mountain village, waterfalls and the turquoise Blue Eye spring.

  6. 06

    Boga e Alpeve · Pejë

    Villages of the Albanian Alps and the Kosovo town of Pejë at the foot of the mountains.

  7. 07

    Drilon · Pogradec · Korçë

    Spring-fed Drilon park, lakeside Pogradec on Lake Ohrid and the old streets of Korçë.

  8. 08

    Përmet · Gjirokastër

    The Vjosa river, Bënja thermal springs and the UNESCO stone city of Gjirokastër.
